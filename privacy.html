Privacy Policy

Emberstep: Dungeon Delve for iPhone · Effective November 23, 2026

Data in the game

The developer does not collect personal data or gameplay data through the app. The game has no login, advertising, analytics, tracking, or online leaderboard. It does not upload your save or settings.

Local saves and deletion

The app stores settings, unlocks, and expedition progress on your iPhone so you can resume play. The local save persists until you delete the app. Apple may include app data in a device backup if you have enabled one. You can manage backups in your Apple settings.

Support and the website

If you email support, we use your email address and the information you send to respond to your request. Support correspondence is retained for up to 180 days after resolution. You can request earlier deletion by emailing lutratally@qq.com. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider handles standard website requests under its privacy statement.

《地牢爬行者》隐私政策

本政策于 2026 年 11 月 23 日生效。开发者不会通过应用收集个人数据或游玩数据。游戏没有登录、广告、统计分析、追踪或在线排行榜，不上传存档和设置。

设置、解锁记录与探险进度保存在本机，用于继续游玩。删除应用会移除本机存档。如果您启用了设备备份，Apple 可能会将应用数据纳入备份，您可在 Apple 设置中管理。

如您主动发送支持邮件，我们会使用您的邮箱和来信信息回复请求。支持邮件在问题解决后最多保留 180 天。您可发送邮件至 lutratally@qq.com 请求提前删除。本站由 GitHub Pages 托管，网站请求按其隐私声明处理。
