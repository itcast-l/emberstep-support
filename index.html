Emberstep: Dungeon Delve

iPhone game support

Getting started

Choose Tutorial on the title screen to practice movement and card combat. Use the six movement buttons to step or turn. Tap the minimap to see the whole floor.

Tap a card to play it. Chain cards in order of increasing mana cost for stronger effects. Hold a card to preview its target and check for a boss counterattack.

Saving your expedition

Progress saves locally as you play. Use the expedition menu to save and return to the title screen, then continue from there. Deleting the app erases its local save. Keep the app installed when troubleshooting an expedition.

Contact

Email lutratally@qq.com for help. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and the steps that led to the issue. For billing or refunds, use Apple Support.
